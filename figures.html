Jane Street ASIC puzzle · 2026

Inside the chip

Recovered from puzzle.gds alone: the design is an 11×11 two-star Star Battle checker. It reads the grid as 121 serial bits and raises success one cycle after the last one.

Contains the solution. Submissions close 4 September 2026 — keep this private until then.

Emitted one ASCII byte per cycle on O[7:0] once success is high. It is an OCaml comment.

Tallies are decoded from the chip’s own accumulator flip-flops, not recomputed. The row counter is one-hot and resets every 11 cycles; the 11 column and 11 region counters are two-bit and saturate at 3.

Where each block sits on the die

Every gate assigned to a recovered block by cone membership, then placed by probing the net at its output pin. The cell counter is the mod-11 one. Die is 200 × 353 µm; grey marks all other logic. The puzzle’s own hint image boxes off one region as the “output generator”: 207 of the 208 cells recovered under that name fall inside that box, as do all 12 of its flip-flops, and no flip-flop from any other block does.

Easter eggs

The two anonymized cell types, INTERNAL_3 and INTERNAL_7, are empty boxes 3 and 9 sites wide. All 36 of their instances sit in a single row below the cell array, and the widths and gaps quantize exactly: dot, dash, element gap, letter gap, word gap. Latin for “through the sand to the stars”, on per aspera ad astra. Sand as in silicon.

Synthesized with yosys onto a Liberty built from this die: cell functions from the SkyWater models, cell areas measured off puzzle.gds. The reconstruction is smaller because it carries no clock tree (the die has 31 clock buffers plus 15 balancing loads) and because the silicon spends flops on a one-hot row tally where the RTL uses two bits. It is packaged for Tiny Tapeout in tinytapeout/, verified through that pinout. The design was written twice, independently, in Verilog and in Hardcaml — Jane Street’s own OCaml hardware language, which is what the payload’s (* … *) is winking at. Both are proved equivalent to the silicon by the same miter. Through the identical synthesis flow they do not converge in size: 451 cells against 363.
